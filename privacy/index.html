What we collect and why

WhereWhatWhy

We only contact you about the request you made, using the channel you agreed to. We never sell your information, and we don't send marketing to anyone who hasn't agreed to receive it.

Who processes it for us

  • GitHub: hosts this website and the chat demo page
  • Supabase: stores form requests in our database, hosted in the European Union
  • Resend: sends us an email alert when a request arrives
  • Anthropic: provides the AI model that writes the chat demo's replies
  • Google: our email provider

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa, including in the European Union and the United States. We use them only for the purposes above and choose providers bound by data protection obligations, as POPIA requires for cross-border transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Requests that don't become work: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted
  • Client records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting
  • Chat demo usage counts: deleted automatically after 30 days; the conversation itself isn't saved
  • If you ask us to stop contacting you, we keep only your number or email on a do-not-contact list so we don't contact you again

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. Email replyfirstdigital@gmail.com or reply "stop" to any message from us. We'll respond within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Security

Our database can only be read by our own server code, never directly from the internet, and access to our accounts is protected. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affected your information, we would tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.
